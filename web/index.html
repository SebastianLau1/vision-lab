What happens on each frame

  1. 01Capture

    A webcam frame or your image is drawn to a canvas, capped at 1920 px.

  2. 02Letterbox

    Scaled into a 640 × 640 grey-padded square and packed into an RGB float tensor.

  3. 03Infer

    YOLO11n runs through ONNX Runtime Web on WebAssembly, on your CPU.

  4. 04Decode

    Top class per anchor, mapped back to image pixels, then class-aware NMS at IoU 0.45.
